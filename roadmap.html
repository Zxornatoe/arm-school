Roadmap

The road to CA, in full

The route from where you are now to qualifying as a Chartered Accountant, and where ARM teaches you along the way.

Home  /  Courses  /  Road to CA

  1. PRC — Pre-Requisite Competencies

    Three papers over two to five months, depending on the student's performance: Fundamentals of Accounting, Quantitative Analysis for Business, and Business and Economic Insights.

  2. CAF — Certificate in Accounting and Finance

    Eight papers in two groups, with weekly marked mocks throughout.

  3. Training contract

    Students register a training contract with a practising firm alongside their studies. Ask us about articleship placement.

  4. CFAP — Certified Finance and Accounting Professional

    Recorded lectures with guidance and mentorship alongside.

  5. Strategic Case Study

    The final assessment, trained with full-length attempts marked against the examiner's grid.

Eligibility

Where do you stand?

To register directly, you need the required percentage in FSc / intermediate, or the required grades in A levels. If you fall short of it, you must first pass ICAP's QAT (Qualifying Assessment Test). With less than 50% in intermediate, you cannot sit the QAT either.

Ask us to check where you stand — send your result and we will map your route.
