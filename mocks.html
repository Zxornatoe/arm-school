Testing system

Mocks marked like the real examiner

Attempt a mock any day of the session. Email your script and it comes back as a detailed PDF — every question marked, every mark reconciled to the total.

Home / Mocks
📚

Marked in context, not by keyword

Law, DSR and case-study answers are read as answers. Your own words are kept, marked green and red with short examiner comments, and judged on the concept rather than on a matching phrase.

📊

Numbers checked working by working

BIA, MA, FAR and Tax scripts are checked calculation by calculation, so you see exactly where a figure went wrong rather than losing a block of marks silently.

✅

Marks that add up

Question marks, totals and percentages reconcile everywhere in the report. A report that does not reconcile is never sent.

⭐

Consistent every time

The same answer to the same mock always receives the same result. Resubmitting an identical script does not change your marks.

Submit your mock

Choose your subject and mock, and send your script in one place. Your marked result is emailed to you.

After sending, attach your script (PDF or clear photos) in the same WhatsApp chat. Your marked report comes back to your email as a detailed PDF.

How it works

One form, one script, one report

  1. Attempt under time

    Sit the mock like the real paper — same timing, no notes.

  2. Send the form

    Fill the subject, mock, CRN, name and the email your result should go to.

  3. Attach your script

    Send the attempted script in the same chat, or email it to armbusinesschool@gmail.com.

  4. Get your report

    Every question marked, every mark reconciled to the total — emailed to you as a PDF.

The proof is in the results

Gold medals and merit certificates from the last two attempts, marked the same way.
