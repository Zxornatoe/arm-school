PRC · 3 papers

Pre-Requisite Competencies

⏰ 2 to 5 months, depending on the student's performance 👥 Online and hybrid

Three papers — the entry level of the CA qualification. PRC runs from two to five months, depending on the student's performance, and covers the accounting, quantitative and business foundations that every later level assumes.

    PRC-1 Fundamentals of Accounting
    Sir Hudaid Wasi · Sir Syed Huzaifa
  • ✓

    Sir Naeem Ullah · Sir Mustafa Tanveer
  • ✓
    PRC-3 Business and Economic Insights
    Sir M. Saad Altaf (business) · Sir Zeeshan Mumtaz (economics)
How to apply Rs 20,000 per subject · Rs 10,000 one-time admission fee

CAF · 8 papers

Certificate in Accounting and Finance

👥 Online and hybrid 📝 Weekly mocks · mid-term · two finals

Eight papers in two groups, taken as a full group or subject by subject. The course finishes at least a month before the examination, leaving room for revision.

CFAP · 6 papers

Certified Finance and Accounting Professional

⏰ Classes begin about two months after the ICAP examinations 👥 Recorded lectures only

Six papers, delivered in recorded format with guidance alongside. CFAP is taught in recorded format; physical classes are not offered at this level.
