PRC · 3 papers

Pre-Requisite Competencies

2 to 5 months, depending on the student's performance  ·  Online and hybrid

Three papers — the entry level of the CA qualification. PRC runs from two to five months, depending on the student's performance, and covers the accounting, quantitative and business foundations that every later level assumes.

  • ✓  PRC-1 Fundamentals of Accounting — Sir Hudaid Wasi · Sir Syed Huzaifa
  • ✓  PRC-2 Quantitative Analysis for Business — Sir Naeem Ullah · Sir Mustafa Tanveer
  • ✓  PRC-3 Business and Economic Insights — Sir M. Saad Altaf (business) · Sir Zeeshan Mumtaz (economics)

How to apply

Rs 20,000 per subject · Rs 10,000 one-time admission fee

CAF · 8 papers

Certificate in Accounting and Finance

Online and hybrid  ·  Weekly mocks · mid-term · two finals

Eight papers in two groups, taken as a full group or subject by subject. The course finishes at least a month before the examination, leaving room for revision.

  • ✓  Group A — CAF-01 Financial Accounting and Reporting — Sir Ahmed Raza Mir · Sir Aakash Kumar
  • ✓  Group A — CAF-02 Taxation Principles and Compliance — Sir Abdullah Maqsood Chaudhary
  • ✓  Group A — CAF-03 Data, Systems and Risks — Sir Ahmed Raza Mir
  • ✓  Group A — CAF-04 Business Law Dynamics — Sir Hudaid Wasi
  • ✓  Group B — CAF-05 Management Accounting — Sir Ahmed Raza Mir
  • ✓  Group B — CAF-06 Corporate Reporting — Sir Ahmed Raza Mir · Sir Aakash Kumar
  • ✓  Group B — CAF-07 Business Insights and Analysis — Sir Ahmed Raza Mir
  • ✓  Group B — CAF-08 Audit and Assurance Essentials — Sir Aakash Kumar

CFAP · 6 papers

Certified Finance and Accounting Professional

Classes begin about two months after the ICAP examinations  ·  Recorded lectures only

Six papers, delivered in recorded format with guidance alongside. CFAP is taught in recorded format; physical classes are not offered at this level.

  • ✓  CFAP-01 Advanced Corporate Reporting — Sir Ahmed Raza Mir
  • ✓  CFAP-02 Corporate Laws and Governance — Sir Hudaid Wasi
  • ✓  CFAP-03 Sustainability Reporting and Assurance — Sir M. Saad Altaf
  • ✓  CFAP-04 Strategic Business Finance — Sir Ahmed Raza Mir
  • ✓  CFAP-05 Tax Practices and Planning — Sir Abdullah Maqsood Chaudhary
  • ✓  CFAP-06 Audit, Assurance and Data — Sir Aakash Kumar
