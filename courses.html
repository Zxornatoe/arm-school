PRC · 3 papers

Pre-Requisite Competencies

2 to 5 months, depending on the student's performance Online and hybrid

Three papers — the entry level of the CA qualification. PRC runs from two to five months, depending on the student's performance, and covers the accounting, quantitative and business foundations that every later level assumes.

  • PRC-1 Fundamentals of AccountingSir Hudaid Wasi · Sir Syed Huzaifa
  • PRC-2 Quantitative Analysis for BusinessSir Naeem Ullah · Sir Mustafa Tanveer
  • PRC-3 Business and Economic InsightsSir M. Saad Altaf (business) · Sir Zeeshan Mumtaz (economics)
How to apply Rs 20,000 per subject · Rs 10,000 one-time admission fee
CAF · 8 papers

Certificate in Accounting and Finance

Online and hybrid Weekly mocks · mid-term · two finals

Eight papers in two groups, taken as a full group or subject by subject. The course finishes at least a month before the examination, leaving room for revision.

  • Group A — CAF-01 Financial Accounting and ReportingSir Ahmed Raza Mir · Sir Aakash Kumar
  • Group A — CAF-02 Taxation Principles and ComplianceSir Abdullah Maqsood Chaudhary
  • Group A — CAF-03 Data, Systems and RisksSir Ahmed Raza Mir
  • Group A — CAF-04 Business Law DynamicsSir Hudaid Wasi
  • Group B — CAF-05 Management AccountingSir Ahmed Raza Mir
  • Group B — CAF-06 Corporate ReportingSir Ahmed Raza Mir · Sir Aakash Kumar
  • Group B — CAF-07 Business Insights and AnalysisSir Ahmed Raza Mir
  • Group B — CAF-08 Audit and Assurance EssentialsSir Aakash Kumar
CAF registration form Rs 18,000 per subject online · Rs 23,000 hybrid
CFAP · 6 papers

Certified Finance and Accounting Professional

Classes begin about two months after the ICAP examinations Recorded lectures only

Six papers, delivered in recorded format with guidance alongside. CFAP is taught in recorded format; physical classes are not offered at this level.

  • CFAP-01 Advanced Corporate ReportingSir Ahmed Raza Mir
  • CFAP-02 Corporate Laws and GovernanceSir Hudaid Wasi
  • CFAP-03 Sustainability Reporting and AssuranceSir M. Saad Altaf
  • CFAP-04 Strategic Business FinanceSir Ahmed Raza Mir
  • CFAP-05 Tax Practices and PlanningSir Abdullah Maqsood Chaudhary
  • CFAP-06 Audit, Assurance and DataSir Aakash Kumar
