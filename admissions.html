Admissions

Registration is online

Once your form is verified, your portal opens within 48 hours.

Home / Admissions

  1. Fill the registration form

    CAF: use the CAF registration form. CFAP: use the CFAP form. For any other level, message us on WhatsApp with your name, level and the subjects you want.

    CAF form CFAP form

  2. Pay the first installment

    The fee is payable in two equal installments. Send the payment proof along with your form.

  3. Get your portal access

    Access is granted within 48 hours of the form being verified, on one registered device.

  4. Join your class group

    Scan the QR code on your student portal to join the official student group.

Where to transfer the fee

Fees can be transferred to the account for your level. Send the payment proof with your registration form.
