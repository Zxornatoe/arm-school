Money matters

Fees

Can the course fee be paid in more than two installments?
No. The fee is strictly payable in two equal installments only.
Is the installment facility available for repeater students?
No. Installments are not permitted on repeater fees, and no discount is offered on repeater fees.
Is any fee discount available?
Fee concessions may be considered strictly on the basis of genuine financial hardship. Students requesting a discount must submit: 1. Details of source of income 2. Latest payslip, if applicable 3. Electricity bills for the last three months 4. Total number of family members All discount requests are subject to management review and approval. Submitting the documents does not guarantee approval.
My friend was given a lower fee. Can I have the same fee?
Fee concessions are not granted on a student-to-student comparison basis. Discounts are assessed individually. If you wish to apply for a concession, please submit the required financial documentation for review.
Is a freeze option available after the result?
Yes. The freeze option is available to students who have paid full or partial fees, provided the request is submitted within the announced deadline. Requests submitted after the specified dates will not be entertained.
What does a CAF subject cost?
For the August 2026 attempt, each CAF subject is Rs 18,000 online and Rs 23,000 hybrid. Business Law Dynamics can also be taken as the Business Law portion or the Company Law portion alone, at Rs 10,000 each.
What is the refresher fee?
Joining from the start of the batch: Rs 8,000 for our own students and Rs 10,000 for outsiders. Joining after the result: Rs 6,000 and Rs 8,000. Customised students are treated as outsider refreshers.
Where do I transfer the fee?
For CFAP: Bank Islami, account title ARM TEACHING SOLUTIONS, account number 120300109280001. For CAF: Meezan Bank, account title ARM Business School, account number 99510114774472.
Study modes

Classes & courses

Will classes be conducted physically, or will recordings be provided?
Both. Students have access to live classes as well as recorded lectures.
What is hybrid mode?
Hybrid mode allows you to attend both physical classes and live online sessions, giving you flexibility in how you study.
What is the difference between online and hybrid?
Online means you attend the live session remotely and watch the recordings. Hybrid lets you move between physical classes and live online sessions as your schedule allows. Hybrid costs Rs 23,000 per CAF subject against Rs 18,000 for online.
If I choose online mode, can I attend mock exams and workshops physically?
Yes. Students enrolled in online mode may attend mock examinations and workshops physically at the campus.
Do you provide recorded classes?
Yes. Recorded lectures of the live sessions are available to students.
Are CFAP classes conducted physically on a daily basis?
No. CFAP classes are conducted in recorded format only. Physical classes are not offered for CFAP.
When will the CAF course be completed?
The course is completed at least one month before the examination date, to leave adequate time for revision and practice. In most cases our teachers finish around 1.5 months before.
How many papers are there in CAF?
Eight, in two groups. Group A is CAF-01 Financial Accounting and Reporting, CAF-02 Taxation Principles and Compliance, CAF-03 Data, Systems and Risks and CAF-04 Business Law Dynamics. Group B is CAF-05 Management Accounting, CAF-06 Corporate Reporting, CAF-07 Business Insights and Analysis and CAF-08 Audit and Assurance Essentials.
What is the English Communication Skills course?
HOC-03 English Communication Skills is a hands-on short course taught by Sir Abdul Basit Khan, alongside the CA papers.
Will a refresher get a new batch or the same lectures?
A refresher joins the running batch and continues with it from that point onward. You are also given a road map to cover all the lectures.
Student portal

Portal

I have submitted the registration form. When will I receive access?
Portal access is granted within 48 hours after your registration form is successfully submitted and verified.
Please add me to the student WhatsApp group.
Scan the QR code available on your student portal to join the official group.
I want to log in on another device. What should I do?
Device access can be reset on request. Contact the administration with your registered email address and we will reset it for you.
Can access be allowed on two devices at the same time?
No. Access is strictly limited to one device per student, to maintain academic integrity and system security.
I logged in on another device and an error is appearing.
Changing devices on your own is not permitted. You may access the portal on one registered device only. Contact the administration if you need a device reset.
My portal has been deactivated. Why?
Portal access is deactivated when there are outstanding dues. Clear the pending payment and complete the required form to restore access.
I am getting a "wrong credentials" error. What should I do?
Copy and paste the exact credentials shared with you, and make sure there is no extra space before or after the username or password.
I am getting an error code while logging in.
Watch the troubleshooting video and follow the steps carefully — if the issue persists, contact the administration office. Play troubleshooting video (YouTube Shorts)
My attempt is after 1.5 years. Until when will access be provided?
Portal access is granted up to your first examination attempt only.
