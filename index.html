About the school

A chartered accountancy teaching institution.

ARM teaches the full route to the CA qualification — PRC, CAF, CFAP and the Strategic Case Study — alongside short hands-on courses, with guidance and mentorship from registration to the examiner's grid.

More about us

Every paper is taught by a specialist, and every mock is marked in detail so a student sees exactly where marks are won and lost. Classes run online and hybrid, and each course finishes well before the examination to leave room for revision.

Merit certificates and gold medals from the Institute of Chartered Accountants of Pakistan sit alongside a testing and marking system built in-house.

Courses

Four levels of the CA qualification, plus short hands-on courses. Open any course to see its papers, teachers and fees.

Pre-Requisite Competencies

PRC · 3 papers. The entry level of the CA qualification — three papers covering the foundations every later level assumes.

Open course

Certificate in Accounting and Finance

CAF · 8 papers. Eight papers in two groups, taken as a full group or subject by subject, with weekly marked mocks.

Open course

Certified Finance and Accounting Professional
