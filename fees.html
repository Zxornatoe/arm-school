Policies

Our official policies.

Two installments only

The course fee is payable in two equal installments. More than two installments are not permitted.

Repeater fees

Installments are not available on repeater fees, and no discount is offered on repeater fees under any circumstances.

Refresher students

A refresher joining from the start of the batch pays Rs 8,000 as our own student and Rs 10,000 as an outsider. Joining after the result, it is Rs 6,000 and Rs 8,000. Customised students do not fall in the own-student category and are treated as outsider refreshers.

Scholarships and concessions

Scholarships are available. Concessions are considered strictly on genuine financial hardship: submit your source of income, the latest payslip where applicable, the last three months' electricity bills, and the total number of family members. All requests go to management review; submitting the documents does not guarantee approval.

No comparison-based discounts

Concessions are assessed individually and are never granted on the basis of what another student was charged.

Freezing a subject

A freeze option is available after the result for students who have paid full or partial fees, provided the request is submitted within the announced deadline. Requests after the deadline are not entertained.

Outstanding dues

Portal access is deactivated while dues are outstanding. Clear the payment and complete the required form to restore access.
